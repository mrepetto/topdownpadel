TOP DOWN PADEL / PRIVACY & ANALYTICS

Privacy, in plain language.

Top Down Padel is developed by Korpos Consulting. This page describes the website and browser testing build. Updated 5 November 2026.

Your game progress

Game progress, preferences and language are stored on your device. Clearing browser storage may remove them. The website and game share the same origin; moving to /game/ does not intentionally reset your progress.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we collect pseudonymous browser/device and session identifiers, approximate device platform, language, game version, active session duration, match events, selected game modes, progression milestones, performance measurements, and website interactions. We do not ask for your name or email for analytics. Referrer hostnames and campaign labels may be recorded; full browsing history is not collected.

Choose “Allow analytics” to help us improve the game, or “No thanks” to keep playing without optional tracking. You can change your choice below. Declining stops new analytics collection and removes the local analytics identifiers; it does not erase game saves or automatically delete previously received records.

Online play and security

Online rooms use the display name you enter and temporary connection information so other players can join. Hosting services process network requests to deliver the game. The private admin area uses a secure authentication cookie and records limited login-attempt information to prevent abuse.

Data use and requests

We use measured activity to understand game performance and improve the experience. We do not sell analytics data or use it for advertising profiles. Contact Korpos Consulting for access, deletion or privacy questions. Raw analytics are retained for up to 90 days, with periodic cleanup. The dashboard explains when available data collection began.

Privacidad y analítica

El progreso se guarda en tu dispositivo. La analítica es opcional: puedes aceptar o rechazarla sin dejar de jugar. Con tu permiso medimos sesiones, actividad de partidas, dispositivo, versión y rendimiento mediante identificadores seudónimos. No vendemos estos datos ni los utilizamos para perfiles publicitarios. Rechazar detiene nuevas mediciones, pero no elimina automáticamente datos recibidos anteriormente. Para consultas o solicitudes de eliminación, contacta con Korpos Consulting.
